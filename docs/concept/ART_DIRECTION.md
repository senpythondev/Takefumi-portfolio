# Art direction — concept references (owner-approved, 2026-11-19)

These four images were generated by the owner as **visual targets**. They are references for mood, composition, light and colour. They are not site assets, and the real-time 3D is not expected to be photoreal. It must, however, read as the same world at first glance.

Do not copy their flaws. The "Do not take" lists below are as binding as the "Take" lists.

---

## 1. `hero-descent.png` — the master reference (hero + whole-site structure)

**Role:** layer 0 (hero) and the scroll structure of the entire site. Scrolling = descending this borehole.

**Take:**
- Composition: the left ~40% is calm dark rock reserved for the headline and chips; the strata and borehole live in the right ~60%. On mobile, the strata sit behind the text, dimmed, with the borehole centred.
- The cyan borehole (= the evidence colour) running straight down. As the visitor scrolls, the camera follows it down; the borehole is the visual thread through the whole site.
- The surface at the top: a thin horizon line, night sky, a tiny drill rig with one warm light. This is "the surface" (layer 0) and the only place the sky appears.
- Thick, wavy sedimentary strata with real depth: layers overlap, overhang and cast shadows. Use parallax between layer depths, not a flat texture.
- **Geodes as windows:** cavities in the strata that each contain a miniature, lit preview of the scene waiting in that layer. Map them to real layers:
  - server racks → layer 5 (live auction)
  - city → layer 4 (legacy PHP audit), styled like `city.png`
  - river of light particles → layer 3 (site migration: 214 posts flowing to the new site)
  When the camera reaches a geode, it flies into it and that geode becomes the full layer scene.
- Light: mostly darkness, with light coming from the borehole and from inside the geodes.

**Do not take:**
- The old-world city with aqueduct arches. Every miniature world must look modern and technical.
- Large, literal fossils (ammonite, trilobite, fern) as focal points. A few faint ones may stay as texture detail only.
- The moon as a bright focal point. Keep it small and dim, or remove it.

## 2. `lantern.png` — the hero interaction

**Role:** what the cursor does on the hero (the flashlight in BRIEF §5-0).

**Take:**
- A soft circular beam of warm light follows the pointer. Only inside the beam does the rock turn partly translucent and reveal what is embedded in the strata: thin cyan circuit traces running like mineral veins, small wireframe structures, points of light.
- Outside the beam the rock is plain and dark. The contrast is the point: the evidence is there, but you have to look for it.
- On touch devices the beam drifts slowly by itself and follows a drag (PLAN B2).

**Do not take:**
- The beam's light source shown in the frame (the bright spot at the top). The beam is the cursor; there is no visible lamp.

## 3. `core-lab.png` — the boring core and the finale

**Role:** (a) the material reference for the boring-core rebuild (PM_REVIEW_08 §1); (b) the final scene, layer 10 "next layer".

**Take:**
- The core itself: long and slender, distinct bands with clear colour boundaries, fine horizontal drilling striations, a polished flat cut face showing the strata, resting on two small supports. This replaces the squat cylinder.
- **Finale:** after the last layer, the completed core is laid on the dark archive table. Above each band rises a thin cyan wireframe hologram of that layer's project (city, server racks, pipeline, timeline…), linked to the band by a thin line. The ruler ticks along the table edge stay.
- One extra band at the end of the core is **empty and faintly glowing**: "the next layer — your project". This is the call to action.

**Do not take:**
- Generic icons (mountains, planet, crystal) that don't correspond to a real project. Every hologram must map to an actual layer in `src/content`.

## 4. `city.png` — layer 4 (legacy PHP security audit)

**Role:** the look of the 45,000-building city (PM_REVIEW_08 §5).

**Take:**
- Night, cold blue-grey buildings, faint warm window dots, fog in the distance and in the valleys.
- An organic, grown layout: irregular districts, dense low old areas against sparse newer towers, curving main roads with light along them.
- **The only warm colour is the threat:** a few buildings glowing red-orange from inside like embers, with thin red cracks of light spreading along the streets toward them. In the scene, the cracks are the attack path being traced. They spread as the visitor moves the threshold slider, and they seal when the files are neutralised.

**Do not take:**
- Photoreal facades. Stylise in the shader (window dots, rim light, fog) within the agreed budgets: no extra draw calls, no extra triangles.

## Textures
Rich rock surface detail may come from **seamless rock textures generated by image AI** (the PM will issue the prompts in `docs/MEDIA_PROMPTS.md`), combined with the existing strata shader. That is cheaper than doing everything procedurally. Until those textures arrive, use procedural placeholders.

## Acceptance for the vertical slice
The owner opens the Vercel preview in their own browser and, at a glance, recognises `hero-descent.png` and `lantern.png` in the hero, and the core from `core-lab.png` in the navigator. "Looks like a tech demo" fails. "Looks like that picture, and it moves" passes.
